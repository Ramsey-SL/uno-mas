## BRAND NAME RULE — CRITICAL · APPLY EVERYWHERE

**The brand is always written "Uno Más" — with the accent on the "a".**

- **Always:** Uno Más · UNO MÁS · uno más
- **Never:** Uno Mas · UNO MAS · uno mas · UnoMas · Uno-Mas (in any human-readable surface)

This applies to **every** surface the brand name appears on: prose, headlines, ad copy, email, SMS, social captions, menus, merch, signage, web pages, artifact titles, SVGs, Canva designs, image alt text, review responses, reports, dashboards, and AI-generated drafts. No exceptions.

**When copying/generating text:** type `Más` — never `Mas`. On Mac, Option+E then A. On Windows, Alt+0225 (á) or Alt+0193 (Á).

**File paths and URLs** may keep ASCII `Uno-Mas` / `uno-mas` for technical compatibility (e.g. `uno-mas-template-hub.netlify.app`) — but any name that displays to humans must use `Más`.

**When reviewing any draft:** if you see `Uno Mas` or `UNO MAS` without the accent, it's wrong. Fix it.

## The Golden Circle (The Soul)

### WHY — Our Purpose
We believe a night out should feel like a discovery, not a transaction. Spokane deserves a place where the food is as good as the vibe — and the vibe is the point.

### HOW — How We Deliver It
- Quarter-pound tacos and house-smoked meats made in-house, not shortcuts
- A two-venue space engineered to feel like you found something (not just went somewhere)
- A community-first approach: loyalty that pays back, events that create regulars, a brand with actual personality
- Kid-friendly at all times — no 21+ window. Atmosphere is curated by design, never by exclusion.

### WHAT — What We Offer
Latin-inspired tacos, house-smoked meats, craft cocktails, a rooftop speakeasy (The Mezzanine), an elevated dinner menu with a Raw Bar, and private event hosting — all at 2020 N Monroe, Spokane.

## The StoryBrand Map

### 1. The Hero (Character)
Spokane locals and transplants, ages 25–45. The person who answers "where should we go?" in the group chat. They want a night worth having — not just food, not just drinks, but an experience they'll tell people about.

**Their desire:** To feel like they found something. To be the person who always knows the best spot.

---

### 2. The Problem (Three Layers)

**External Problem** *(the task)*
"I need somewhere to go tonight that's actually worth it."

**Internal Problem** *(the feeling)*
The low-grade stress of being the one who has to pick the place — and the fear of letting everyone down with a forgettable choice. Boredom with the same rotation. FOMO when it sounds like everyone else found something better.

**Philosophical Problem** *(the injustice)*
You shouldn't have to choose between great food and a great vibe. Most places give you one or the other. That's a false choice — and Uno Más refuses to accept it.

---

### 3. The Guide (Uno Más)

**Guide Positioning: Option A — Peer / Group-Chat Energy**
*We've been there too. We built the answer.*

Uno Más doesn't talk down to the customer or position itself as above them. We're the friend who already found the good spot — and we're texting you about it. The voice is warm, confident, and already in on the joke. We empathize as a peer, not a brand.

**Empathy** *(rooted in our WHY)*
You've been to the place that sounded good and wasn't. We have too. That's the whole reason Uno Más exists — so the next time someone asks "where should we go?" you don't have to think twice.

**Authority** *(rooted in our HOW)*
- Top organic posts: 1,400+ likes, 76K views — people share us because we're worth sharing
- Loyalty members spend 107% more and visit twice as often
- Quarter-pound tacos made in-house. Not reheated. Not pre-portioned.
- Spokane's only converted garage speakeasy with a full dinner program and Raw Bar

**In Practice — Guide Voice Examples:**
- "The group chat is asking where to go. You already know the answer."
- "We'll see you at 2020 N Monroe."
- "The spot the group chat keeps landing on."
- "So the next time someone asks — you've got an answer."

---

### 4. The Plan (Three Steps)

**Step 1 — Show Up**
Walk in. No reservation required. 2020 N Monroe, behind Indaba Coffee. Easy parking.

**Step 2 — Get Lost**
Order something unexpected. Try the Feast. Go upstairs. Let the night take a turn.

**Step 3 — Come Back**
Join Uno Más Rewards: The Cantina Club. Get rewarded. Become a regular. You're the person who always knows where to go now.

---

### 5. The Call to Action

**Direct CTA:** Come in tonight. Walk-ins always welcome.
**Transitional CTA:** See the full menu at unomastacoshop.com

---

### 6. The Cost of Inaction (Failure Stakes)

Another forgettable Tuesday. Cold delivery tacos and a $15 fee. The same restaurant rotation. A private event in a beige banquet room. The low-grade disappointment of picking the wrong place — again.

---

### 7. The Transformed Life (Success Vision)

You walk in and immediately know this was the right call. The table orders the Feast. Someone orders the Espresso Margarita and doesn't stop talking about it. The group stays two hours longer than planned. On the way out someone says "we have to come back." You become the person who found Uno Más before everyone else did — and now you're the one people ask.

## The Grunt Test (Headline Audit Standard)

Every headline, ad, and hero section must answer these three questions within 3 seconds:

1. **What do you offer?** → Quarter-pound tacos, house-smoked meats, craft cocktails, speakeasy upstairs
2. **How does it make my life better?** → A night you'll actually remember. The spot you'll want to tell people about.
3. **What do I need to do?** → Make a reservation or walk in. 2020 N Monroe.

**Official positioning statement (April 2026):**
> Uno Más is a modern Mexican restaurant and tequila bar in Spokane where the food is serious, the atmosphere is alive, and the only thing we take lightly is ourselves.

**Primary tagline** *(use intentionally, not as filler)*:
> Get a little lost at Uno Más.

---

## Narrative Sequence (Email & Ad Copy Structure)

When writing emails, ads, or longer copy, follow this sequence:

1. **Character + Desire** — Open with the customer's moment ("You need somewhere to go tonight.")
2. **Problem** — Name the internal feeling first, then the external situation
3. **Guide Entry** — Position Uno Más as the one who gets it (empathy) + has earned it (authority)
4. **The Plan** — Make the next step feel obvious and low-risk
5. **CTA** — Direct and specific
6. **Stakes** — Remind them what they're avoiding (briefly — "the salt in the recipe")
7. **Success** — End on the transformed moment
