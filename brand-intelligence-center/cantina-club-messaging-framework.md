## 1. WHY the club exists
**For the guest (DRAFT):** The people who keep choosing Uno Más should be treated like regulars, not transactions — rewarded for showing up, remembered when they do.
**For the business (DRAFT):** Drive frequency + spend, build an owned audience (email/SMS), and create loyalty that's hard to leave. *(Proof: members spend 107% more and visit 2×.)*
- ❓ **NEEDS RAMSEY:** In one sentence, the *feeling* you want a member to walk away with?

## 2. The Hero — who it's for
**DRAFT:** Free tier = everyone who walks in (Spokane locals/transplants, 25–45, the "group chat" decider). Premium = your most engaged regulars who want status + experience.
- ❓ **NEEDS RAMSEY:** Which growth goal leads — **convert first-timers into regulars**, or **deepen your best regulars** (upsell frequency/spend/events)? (Shapes what we emphasize.)

## 3. The Problem it solves (guest POV)
**DRAFT:** Most loyalty programs are junk — points that never add up, another app nobody opens. Uno Más gives real money back, instantly, with nothing to download — just your phone number.
- ❓ Confirm this is the enemy we position against.

## 4. Positioning — the core idea
**DRAFT:** *Belonging over bargains* — but the **free tier leads with tangible value** ($10 to join, 10% back) as the on-ramp; **premium sells status, access, and experience** (the belonging layer).
- ❓ **NEEDS RAMSEY:** For the **free tier**, lead with **value** ("$10 + 10% back") or **belonging** ("become a regular")? *(Recommendation: value as the hook, belonging as the story.)*

## 6. Message pillars (everything ladders to these — DRAFT, confirm/reprioritize)
1. **Real value, instantly** — $10 just for joining, 10% back.
2. **Effortless** — no app, no card, just your phone number.
3. **Belonging** — become a regular; "always uno más."
4. **It grows with you** — bigger perks coming for the regulars *(sets up premium)*.

## 7. Perks & Benefits — the LADDER
### FREE tier — LIVE (Toast)
- **$10 just for joining** (100-pt signup bonus)
- **1 point per $1**, **$10 back every 100 points (10%)**
- **$10 birthday reward**
- *(optional)* Free-item reward at 100 pts — ❓ name it (e.g., "1 Free House Margarita" / "Free Chips & Guac")
- Members-only offers + first word on specials via email/SMS
- ❓ Do points expire? (confirm Toast setting)

### PREMIUM tiers — TO DESIGN (candidates from prior Cantina Club planning)
Since the FREE tier already gives 10% back + $10 birthday, premium has to be clearly worth paying for. Candidate perks to assign to paid tiers:
- Member drink pricing: **$6 margaritas · $5 pints · $30 pitchers**
- **Monthly dining credit** (e.g., $10/$25/$50 by tier)
- **Food % discount** (10% / 20%)
- **Monthly giveaway** entries (1×/2×/3×) + weekly flash giveaways
- **Status objects:** numbered keychain, custom glassware
- **The Mezzanine:** priority booking + member rates
- **Access:** early/priority events, new-menu tastings, chef's counter, guest bartender, direct event line
- ❓ **NEEDS RAMSEY:** which of these are premium vs. stay free; how many paid tiers; names; monthly/annual prices (prior: $25/$50/$99 · founding $225/$450/$900 — keep?); and the **single reason** a guest upgrades when the free tier already pays 10% back.
