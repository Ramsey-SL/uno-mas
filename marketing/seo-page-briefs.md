## 1. How priority is scored

**Opportunity = Demand × Winnability**, where Winnability = low competition + topical relevance + how much of the SERP we can control (own page vs. directory-dominated).

| Tier | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **P0 — Quick win** | Real demand **and** low competition **and** we have a natural commercial page → rank in 1–3 months. Fund the matching paid campaign until then. |
| **P1 — Build** | Real demand but harder SERP (Map Pack / directories / mid-comp) → 3–6 months, leans on GBP + reviews + content. |
| **P2 — Long game / editorial** | Reputation & discovery terms the SERP gives to listicles/directories → win via editorial content + off-page (GBP, reviews, Inlander, Visit Spokane), not a single commercial page. |

**The big strategic truth from the data:** the highest-*demand* terms ("best restaurants in spokane," "restaurants near me") are **off-page games** (Map Pack, reviews, third-party "best of" lists) — on-page SEO alone won't win them. The highest-*winnability* terms (`dinner spokane`, `mexican restaurant spokane`, `birria tacos spokane`, `private dining spokane`, `taco catering`, `north monroe restaurants`, `brunch spokane`) are **cheap quick wins** with a dedicated page. **Build the quick wins; play the long game on reputation through GBP + reviews.**

## 5. The off-page + GBP + reviews layer (wins the high-demand reputation terms)

On-page can't win `best restaurants in spokane` / `restaurants near me` / `top restaurants spokane` alone — those SERPs are **Map Pack + directories + listicles**. Priority order:

1. **🥇 Google Business Profile — claim & optimize** (Place ID still unconfirmed). Correct categories (Mexican restaurant, Tequila bar, Event venue, Caterer), all attributes ("locally owned," "private dining," "catering"), photos, weekly **GBP posts** (Taco Tuesday!), Q&A, products/menu. *Single biggest lever for every "near me" + "best…spokane" + Map Pack result.*
2. **Reviews engine** — automated ask-for-review via Toast/Klaviyo → Google + Yelp. Review **volume + recency + responses** is what ranks you in "best…spokane." Respond to all, signed "The Uno Más Team," under 100 words.
3. **Third-party "best of" + directories** — pitch *Inlander* Best Of, *Visit Spokane*, Yelp/TripAdvisor (claim + maintain), Eventective/The Knot/WeddingWire (events+catering backlinks), Restaurantji, Apple Business Connect, Bing Places.
4. **Citations/NAP consistency** — identical Name/Address/Phone everywhere.
