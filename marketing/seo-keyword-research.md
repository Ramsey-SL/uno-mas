## 🎯 Top-line strategic takeaways

1. **The brand intel competitor list is incomplete.** Missing the most direct competitor: **Table 13 Tacos & Tequila** at the Davenport Grand Hotel — same positioning as us (tacos + tequila), downtown anchor location, large tequila list as their hook. Add to master competitor list immediately.

2. **"Modern Mexican Spokane" is already contested.** Purgatory Agave actively claims this phrase ("modern Latin design," "blends rustic charm with modern Latin"). Cochinito Taqueria owns "chef-driven taqueria." Mole owns "authentic / celebrated Mexican kitchens." To win this category, we differentiate on **room + experience + three venues**, not the word "modern."

3. **🚀 BIG OPENING — Spokane brunch is a Mexican-shaped hole.** Top Sunday brunch results (Bruncheonette, Chaps, Italia Trattoria, 1898 Public House, House of Brunch) are ALL non-Mexican. There is no "Mexican brunch in Spokane" answer right now. Uno Más could **own this lane outright** when brunch launches — but only with schema markup, llms.txt, and dedicated content ready BEFORE the launch.

4. **Speakeasy field is thin.** Twenty-Seventh Heaven (event-only, no food), Durkins (historic), Cease and Desist Book Club. **No competitor combines speakeasy + working restaurant downstairs + private events** — The Mezzanine wins this combination if we position it that way.

5. **Top organic "best Mexican Spokane" results are dominated by directory sites** (Yelp, TripAdvisor, Restaurantji). Direct restaurant sites barely show up. The fastest path to rank is:
   - Strong Google Business Profile (most important — drives local pack)
   - Schema markup that lets us appear in restaurant cards + AI Overviews
   - Yelp + TripAdvisor claim + maintenance
   - llms.txt that disambiguates our positioning for AI crawlers

6. **No competitor has strong, visible JSON-LD schema** in their HTML when we look at their search result snippets. Implementing comprehensive Restaurant + Menu + Event + LocalBusiness schema is a **quick-win competitive moat** — Google AI Overviews and Perplexity rely heavily on structured data.

## 🤖 AI Search positioning (Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, ChatGPT)

AI search engines pull answers from:
1. **Structured data (JSON-LD)** — Restaurant, Menu, Event, LocalBusiness schemas
2. **High-authority third-party listings** — Yelp, TripAdvisor, Google Business Profile, Visit Spokane
3. **Clearly-stated facts on the website** — address, hours, phone, signature dishes, capacity
4. **llms.txt** (emerging — Anthropic, OpenAI, Perplexity all crawl it) — disambiguates positioning

### Required JSON-LD schema (priority order)

| Schema | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Restaurant** (root entity) | `/` | Powers Google Maps + AI Overviews for "best Mexican Spokane" |
| **LocalBusiness** with OpeningHoursSpecification | `/` , `/contact` | Hours show in Google search panel |
| **Menu + MenuSection + MenuItem** | `/menu`, `/menu/dinner`, etc. | Powers Google menu cards |
| **Event** (when site_events populated) | `/` future, `/private-events` | Powers Google event cards |
| **Service** (for catering) | `/private-events` | Surfaces catering in service searches |
| **FAQPage** | `/about`, `/private-events`, `/contact` | Powers "People also ask" snippets |
| **BreadcrumbList** | All pages | Standard SEO hygiene |
| **WebSite** with SearchAction | `/` | Enables sitelinks search box in Google |
