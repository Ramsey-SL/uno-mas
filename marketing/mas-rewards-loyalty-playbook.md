## 1) The program — your ACTUAL Toast config

| Setting | Current value | Toast field |
|---|---|---|
| Earn rate | **1 point per $1 spent** | Points per dollar |
| Core reward | **$10 off at 100 points** = **10% back** | Reward / points threshold |
| Sign-up bonus | **100 points ($10)** — effectively **$10 just for joining** | Sign-up bonus |
| Birthday | **$10 off during birth month** | Birthday treat |
| Enrollment | **Phone number at checkout** | Signup method |
| (Optional) Free Item Reward | At 100 pts — needs a **name + eligible item** to save | Free Item Reward |
| Redemption | In-shop by phone lookup | POS |

**The hook is "$10 just for signing up."** Lead every message and every server pitch with it — it's far stronger than a free appetizer.

**Economics note:** 10% back + $10 to join + $10 birthday is very generous — perfect for a hard enrollment push. Each signup is ~$10 in reward liability if redeemed. If margins tighten later, dial the core reward to $5/100 (5%) or raise the signup threshold. For now, generosity = velocity.

**Free Item Reward (finish setup):** name it something lovable + low-cost — **"1 Free House Margarita"** or **"Free Chips & Guac"** — set eligible item(s), keep at 100 pts as an alternate redemption to the $10 off.

**Turn these ON to steer traffic (both currently OFF):**
- **Day & time bonus → 2× points** during **Sunday brunch** and/or slow weeknights — drives visits exactly when you want them.
- **Dining option bonus → 2× points** on Toast online/takeout — if you want to grow online orders.

## 5) Email + SMS flows

### A) Launch / boost (existing list — one-time)
**Email** — Subject: `You've got $10 waiting 💵`
> Meet **Más Rewards** — our free loyalty program. **Sign up and get $10 on us**, then earn $10 back for every $100, plus $10 off on your birthday. Next visit, tell your server your phone number.
> **[See how it works →]([/rewards link])**
**SMS:**
> Uno Mas: Join Mas Rewards (free) & get $10 just for signing up + $10 back per $100. Ask your server next visit. Reply STOP to opt out.

### B) Welcome (Toast automated — on enroll)
**SMS, immediately:**
> Welcome to Mas Rewards 🌮 You've got $10 to spend just for joining. Earn $10 back for every $100 after. See you soon!
**Email, +1 day:** how it works + "come use your $10."

### C) "Almost there" nudge (near a reward)
> Uno Mas: You're just [X] points from $10 off 🪙 Come get closer this week - tacos & margs are calling. [reserve link]

### D) "Reward ready" (reward earned)
> Uno Mas: You've got $10 waiting 💵 Redeem on your next visit - just give your phone number. Reply STOP to opt out.

### E) Birthday (Toast automated — birth month)
> Happy birthday from Uno Mas! 🎂 $10 off is on us this month. Come celebrate - reserve: [link].

### F) Win-back (no visit 45 days)
> Uno Mas: Your points miss you 🌮 Come back this week for [bonus]. [reserve link]. Reply STOP to opt out.

**Toast mapping:** Welcome, Almost-there, Reward-ready, Birthday, Win-back = Toast Loyalty automated triggers; the launch (A) = one-time scheduled Email+SMS.
