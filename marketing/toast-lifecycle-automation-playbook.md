## Objective hierarchy

1. **Primary — increase visit frequency.** Drive the 2nd and 3rd visit.
2. **Secondary — convert regulars into the Club.**

Use **Toast-native audience logic as the trigger layer** and keep guest-facing copy human and
on-brand. Automation should drive a *visit*, not just deliver a coupon.

## Operating rules

- **Identify / check in known guests whenever possible.** The data only works if staff capture it.
- Make signup and check-in easy for staff at **both arrival and checkout** — that's the whole game.
- **Measure incremental visits and repeat behavior**, not opens and clicks.
- **Review performance at 30 days, then monthly.**
- Never reward or gate on positive public reviews (automation #8 is follow-up only).
