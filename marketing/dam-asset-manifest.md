**Cloud name:** `drxrfyq9i` · **URL pattern:** `https://res.cloudinary.com/drxrfyq9i/image/upload/<public_id>.png` (images) · `/video/upload/<public_id>.mov` (videos)
ChatGPT (Uno Más Art Director) + Gemini are connected to this DAM — they can **pull these by public_id or by tag**, use them as reference images, or composite the logo into a design.

**Search by tag (Cloudinary search expression):** `tags:watchparty`, `tags:mezzanine`, `tags:cocktails`, `tags:food`, `tags:logo`, `tags:promo`, `tags:events`, `tags:approved AND tags:website`.
