# The Mezzanine — Brand Identity Guidelines

> **Source:** "Mezzanine Branding Cheatsheet.pdf" (filed in Drive `01_Brand_Assets/Logos/Mezzanine/`).
> **The Mezzanine is a SUB-BRAND with its own identity** — moody desert speakeasy. **Never mix Mezzanine and
> Uno Más brand elements in one design.** Use this system for the Mezzanine section of the site, private-event
> collateral, and any Mezzanine-specific creative. Formal name: **"The Mezzanine on Monroe."**

## Colors
| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Electric Pink** | `#E22790` | Primary accent, headlines, CTAs |
| **Magenta** | `#BF28BF` | Secondary accent, gradient mid |
| **Ultra Violet** | `#93009B` | Deep accent, gradient end |
| **Charcoal** | `#333333` | Dark surfaces / text on light |
| **Deep Black** | `#000000` | Primary background / foundation |
| **White** | `#FFFFFF` | Text on dark, logo |

**Signature gradient:** Ultra Violet → Magenta → Electric Pink (`#93009B → #BF28BF → #E22790`).
**Foundation is dark** (deep black / charcoal). This is the opposite of the Uno Más warm/light cantina palette.

## Fonts
- **Titles:** **DIN Condensed VF** — Demi Bold / Regular / Light. *(Web substitute when DIN isn't licensed: Oswald or Saira Condensed.)*
- **H2 + Body:** **Poppins** — Black / Bold / Regular / Medium / Light / Thin.
- **Accent callouts:** **Baka Too** (handwritten script) — use sparingly for personality lines.

## Logo
- **"UM MEZZANINE"** — a UM monogram built from **line-art / painted-desert patterning** (tribal-meets-desert linework) above the word **MEZZANINE** (DIN condensed).
- **Iterations:** white, electric pink, magenta, ultra violet — on both light and dark. Also a **horizontal lockup** (UM + MEZZANINE) and the **UM monogram** alone.
- **On dark backgrounds (default): white or electric-pink logo.** Never recolor outside the palette; never AI-render it.
- Mezzanine logo files: Drive `01_Brand_Assets/Logos/Mezzanine/` (⚠️ not yet in the Cloudinary DAM — upload as a follow-up so the website/AI tools can pull it).

## Accent elements
- **Line-art illustrations:** single-line cacti, agave, succulents, prickly pear, and **layered mountain/desert ranges** (in white, electric pink, magenta, or ultra violet).
- **Painted Desert accents (match the in-venue mural):** greyscale painted desert scenery (saguaro cacti, rocks) + **watercolor backgrounds** in pink/magenta/violet washes.
- Pair dark foundation + a watercolor or line-art accent + one pop of electric pink. Less is more — atmospheric, secret, premium.

## Voice (carries over from brand voice)
Atmospheric, mysterious, less-is-more. "You should know this exists." Fewer adjectives, more scene-setting. 0–1 emoji. Never casual/loud Uno Más energy.

## Web application (the Mezzanine homepage section + /mezzanine)
- **Background:** deep black / charcoal (switch away from the light cantina sections).
- **Headline:** DIN-condensed substitute (Oswald), white; accent words in electric pink `#E22790`.
- **Body:** Poppins.
- **Accents:** line-art cactus/mountain motifs + a violet→pink watercolor glow; signature gradient on CTAs.
- **Imagery:** real Mezzanine photos from the DAM (`mezzanine/venue`): `20251028_MEZZ_VENUE_MezzWatchParty_v5`, `MezzLounge_RAW`, `MezzBar_v2`, `MezzTV_RAW`, `MezzMural_RAW`.
