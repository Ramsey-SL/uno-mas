# Uno Más — Typography

## Overview

Uno Más uses different typeface systems for its two brands. Both are available in Canva through their respective brand kits.

---

## Uno Más — Typeface System

### Primary Fonts

| Role | Font | Style |
|---|---|---|
| Display / Headlines | Antonio | Bold |
| Body / Subheadings | Montserrat | Regular / SemiBold / Bold |

### Antonio — Display Font
- Use for: Large headlines, event names, menu item callouts, campaign headlines, signage
- Character: Strong, condensed, assertive — high visual impact at large sizes
- Never use at body text size (below 18px) — it's a display font only
- All caps preferred for maximum impact in headline contexts
- Pair with Montserrat at smaller sizes for contrast and readability

### Montserrat — Body Font
- Use for: Body copy, subheadings, captions, button labels, SMS/email body
- Character: Clean, modern, versatile — works at any size
- Weight guidelines:
  - **Regular (400):** Body copy, descriptions, secondary information
  - **SemiBold (600):** Subheadings, callouts, emphasis within body
  - **Bold (700):** CTAs, labels, key data points
- Never use Montserrat italic in formal brand contexts — stick to upright styles

## The Mezzanine — Typeface System

### Primary Fonts

| Role | Font | Style |
|---|---|---|
| Display / Headlines | DIN Condensed | Bold |
| Body / Subheadings | Poppins | Regular / Medium / SemiBold |

### DIN Condensed — Display Font
- Use for: Mezzanine headlines, event announcements, atmosphere labels
- Character: Industrial, precise, confident — feels elevated and slightly mysterious when set in Electric Pink on black
- All caps preferred in headline applications
- Pairs well with dark backgrounds and dramatic photography

### Poppins — Body Font
- Use for: Mezzanine body copy, package descriptions, inquiry forms, email body
- Character: Geometric, slightly warmer than DIN — softens the coldness of the display font
- Weight guidelines:
  - **Regular (400):** Body copy and descriptions
  - **Medium (500):** Subheadings and callouts
  - **SemiBold (600):** CTAs and labels

## Hierarchy Rules (Both Brands)

1. Headlines should always be the visually dominant element — never let body copy compete
2. Limit each design to two font weights maximum per zone (e.g., Bold + Regular)
3. Minimum body font size: 14px digital / 10pt print
4. Line height: 1.4–1.6x for body copy; 1.1–1.2x for headlines
5. Letter spacing: tight on headlines (−0.02em to 0em); default on body

---

## What to Avoid

- Do not substitute system fonts (Arial, Helvetica, Times) in any branded material
- Do not mix Uno Más and Mezzanine typefaces in the same design
- Do not use more than two typeface families in any single piece
- Do not use decorative or script fonts unless explicitly approved for a specific campaign
- Do not stretch or compress fonts horizontally — use a condensed font instead
