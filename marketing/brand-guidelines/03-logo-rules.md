# Uno Más — Logo Rules

## Two Brands, Two Logo Systems

Uno Más and The Mezzanine each have their own logo system. They are never interchangeable. Each has specific rules for usage, placement, and color application.

---

## Uno Más Logo

### Approved Color Versions

| Version | Usage |
|---|---|
| Pink on White | Default for digital, print on light backgrounds |
| Pink on Navy | Preferred for dark backgrounds, packaging, merchandise |
| White on Navy | Alternate dark background version |
| Black on White | Single-color print applications only |
| White on Pink | Limited use — promotional, high-energy moments |

### Clear Space
- Maintain a minimum clear space equal to the height of the "U" in "Uno" on all four sides of the logo
- No text, imagery, or other graphic elements may appear within the clear space

### Minimum Size
- Digital: 120px wide minimum
- Print: 1 inch wide minimum
- Below these sizes, use the logomark (icon only) if available

### Placement Dos
- Top-left or centered for most layouts
- Bottom corner for co-branded or sponsored contexts
- Full-width for hero/splash screens (with adequate padding)

### Placement Don'ts
- Do not place on busy photographic backgrounds without a defined container or overlay
- Do not place the Uno Más logo directly adjacent to the Mezzanine logo without intentional co-brand treatment
- Do not rotate, skew, or apply perspective to the logo

---

## The Mezzanine Logo

### Approved Color Versions

| Version | Usage |
|---|---|
| Electric Pink on Black | Primary — default for all Mezzanine applications |
| White on Black | Alternate — single-color or embossed applications |
| Black on Off-White | Print only — when black background is not available |

### Clear Space
- Same rule as Uno Más: clear space equal to the cap height of the logo wordmark on all sides

### Minimum Size
- Digital: 120px wide minimum
- Print: 1 inch wide minimum

### Placement Rules
- Always on dark backgrounds in digital contexts
- When used in Uno Más materials (e.g., "Upstairs at Uno Más"), size the Mezzanine logo subordinate to the Uno Más logo
- The Mezzanine logo should never appear in Pink `#E22690` — its pink is `#E22790` (slightly shifted)

---

## Universal Logo Rules (Both Brands)

### Never Do the Following
- Never stretch, compress, or distort either logo
- Never retype the logo in a different font — always use the approved logo file
- Never apply drop shadows, outlines, glows, or effects to the logo
- Never use the logo at low resolution (blurry or pixelated)
- Never use unapproved color combinations
- Never overlay the logo on a background that creates insufficient contrast
- Never use the logo as a watermark on photography at low opacity

### File Formats
- **Digital use:** SVG (preferred for scalability) or PNG with transparent background
- **Print use:** PDF or EPS
- **Social media:** PNG at 1:1 ratio on appropriate background

### Asset Location
Logo files are stored in: `brand_assets/logos/uno_mas/` and `brand_assets/logos/mezzanine/`

### Co-Brand Situations
When both logos must appear together (e.g., "The Mezzanine at Uno Más"):
- Maintain equal or hierarchically clear sizing
- Use a dividing rule or adequate space between the two logos
- Do not overlap the logos
