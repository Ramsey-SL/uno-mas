# Uno Más — Brand Colors

## Overview

Uno Más operates two distinct visual identities under one roof. The main restaurant (Uno Más) uses a warm, energetic palette that reflects its Latin-inspired taco shop personality. The Mezzanine uses a darker, more dramatic palette that fits its speakeasy character.

---

## Uno Más — Primary Brand Palette

| Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Hot Pink | `#E22690` | Primary accent, CTAs, logo, key headlines |
| Electric Blue | `#18BCDC` | Secondary accent, highlights, links |
| Navy | `#003366` | Dark backgrounds, text on light, structure |
| Yellow | `#FFEC00` | Accent, callouts, energy moments |

### Usage Rules — Uno Más Colors
- **Hot Pink** (`#E22690`) is the brand's most recognizable color. Use it for primary CTAs, headers, and anywhere the brand needs to make a strong first impression.
- **Electric Blue** (`#18BCDC`) pairs with Pink as a complement. Use for secondary elements, icons, and highlights — never compete it with Pink at equal weight.
- **Navy** (`#003366`) is the backbone color for text, backgrounds, and structural elements. Most body copy and dark backgrounds should use Navy.
- **Yellow** (`#FFEC00`) is a high-energy accent. Use sparingly to create moments of emphasis — it should never dominate a layout.

## The Mezzanine — Primary Brand Palette

| Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Electric Pink | `#E22790` | Primary accent, logo, headlines |
| Ultra Violet | `#93009B` | Secondary accent, atmosphere elements |
| Black | `#000000` | Dominant background color |
| Off-White | `#F5F5F5` | Text on dark backgrounds only |

### Usage Rules — Mezzanine Colors
- **Black** dominates all Mezzanine layouts. The space is dark, intimate, and dramatic — the palette should reflect that.
- **Electric Pink** (`#E22790`) is the only warm pop in the Mezzanine palette. Use it for the logo, key headline moments, and primary CTAs.
- **Ultra Violet** (`#93009B`) adds depth and mystery. Use it in gradients, background texture, or supporting design elements.
- **Never use Uno Más Yellow or Blue in Mezzanine contexts** — the palettes are intentionally separate.

## Cross-Brand Rules

- Never mix Uno Más and Mezzanine palettes in a single piece unless specifically creating a unified brand asset.
- When producing content for both brands in a campaign, design two separate versions.
- For print and high-resolution digital: always use the exact hex values above. Do not eyedrop from screenshots.
- For accessibility: ensure at minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio for body text against any background.

---

## Informal Color Reference

When briefing designers or describing colors in copy:

- Hot Pink = "the Uno Más pink"
- Electric Blue = "the Uno Más blue"
- Navy = "the dark background blue"
- Yellow = "the accent yellow"
- Ultra Violet = "the Mezzanine purple"
