# Uno Más — Venue Context

## The Big Picture

Uno Más Tacos & Tequila operates out of a single location in Spokane, WA — but it contains three distinct experiences: the Cantina (main floor), the Patio, and The Mezzanine. Each space has its own identity, its own audience moment, and its own content strategy.

## Venue 1: The Cantina (Main Floor)

### What It Is
The heart of Uno Más. This is the primary dining room — where most guests eat, drink, and spend their time. It's warm, energetic, and community-first. The Cantina is where the food gets made and served, where the bar operates during regular hours, and where the Uno Más brand lives most visibly.

### The Atmosphere
- Warm, colorful, busy during peak hours
- The TACOS neon sign is an iconic visual element
- Main bar runs the full cocktail menu during open hours
- Loud enough to feel alive, controlled enough to have a conversation
- Energy: neighborhood restaurant that happens to have a great bar

### Who Goes Here
- First-time guests discovering Uno Más
- Lunch regulars (Tuesday–Saturday, 11am–5pm window)
- Evening social diners — groups, dates, post-work crews
- Anyone here for the food first, drinks second (or equal)

### Content Strategy for the Cantina
- Food photography is primary — the Cantina is where the food lives
- Atmosphere shots during dinner service show the space at its best
- Bar shots work for cocktail / weekly-specials content
- The TACOS sign is always a good backdrop
- Never: Empty room shots. The Cantina looks best alive.

### Copy Language for the Cantina
- "On Monroe" (casual location reference)
- "Come in" (preferred over "visit us")
- "The main floor" (internal reference)
- "2020 N Monroe" (for ads and first-visit discovery only)
- Do not use "restaurant" in casual copy — we're a taco shop and tequila bar.

## Venue 2: The Patio

### What It Is
The outdoor extension of Uno Más. The patio is seasonal — when it's open, it's one of the best spots on Monroe Street. String lights, open air, and the outdoor vibe that makes warm-weather evenings in Spokane feel like an event.

### The Atmosphere
- String lights overhead
- Table seating, al fresco dining energy
- Warmer and more relaxed than the indoor main floor
- Distinctly seasonal — when it's open, that's news worth announcing

### When It's Open
Patio availability is weather and season-dependent. Patio opening day is always a content moment — treat it like a launch.

### Who Goes Here
- Weekend regulars who want outdoor seating
- Date-night guests
- Groups who want a more casual, outdoor vibe
- Anyone who specifically mentions the patio in reviews or DMs

### Content Strategy for the Patio
- The patio opening each season is a major content moment: email blast, SMS, social post
- String lights in the background of photography are a strong visual signal
- Best shot times: late afternoon or dusk (golden hour hits the string lights perfectly)
- "Patio szn" is an acceptable informal reference in casual social copy

### Copy Language for the Patio
- "The patio is open. ☀️" — classic, always works
- "Margaritas + Monroe Street tonight" — pairing the drink with the location
- "Come get lost on the patio" — references the brand tagline
- "Perfect patio weather" — use only when weather actually warrants it (don't manufacture urgency)

## Venue 3: The Mezzanine

### What It Is
An upstairs speakeasy-style event space above the Uno Más main floor. Most guests who come to the main floor don't know the Mezzanine exists — and that's deliberately part of the brand. It's accessed from within Uno Más and functions as a semi-private or fully private space depending on the booking.

The Mezzanine operates under its own visual identity (see Brand Colors and Typography docs) and its own voice (darker, more atmospheric, less urgent).

### The Atmosphere
- Fireplace as the centerpiece — always the first detail to mention
- Leather lounge seating
- Private bar (upstairs bar, separate from the main floor)
- 28 seats in the main lounge configuration
- Dumbwaiter connecting to the Uno Más kitchen
- Dark, intimate, intentional — the lighting is low, the vibe is deliberate
- Late night: the Mezzanine stays active after the main floor slows down

### Capacity
- **Lounge / cocktail style:** Up to 37 people
- **Seated dinner:** 28 people
- **Minimum for full buyout:** 15 people

### The Three Event Packages

| Package | Capacity | Price |
|---|---|---|
| The Essentials | 15–25 people | $750 (Sun–Thu) / $850 (Fri–Sat) |
| The Fiesta | 20–30 people | $1,200 (Sun–Thu) / $1,400 (Fri–Sat) |
| The Full Send | 25–37 people | $1,800 (Sun–Thu) / $2,000 (Fri–Sat) + $200 bar tab included |

**Note on pricing in content:** Never include package prices in organic social posts. Prices are shared after direct inquiry via DM, email, or call. Email campaigns may reference price ranges.

### Who Books the Mezzanine
- Birthday parties (primary)
- Corporate / team dinners
- Rehearsal dinners
- Holiday parties
- Groups wanting a private experience without renting a full venue
- Late-night groups looking for a scene after the main floor winds down

### Content Strategy for the Mezzanine
- The "secret" angle is the hook: "Most people don't know the Mezzanine exists."
- Feature the fireplace first — it's the most evocative detail
- Event inquiry posts should go on Thursday or Friday (when people plan weekend and upcoming events)
- Monthly minimum: 1 Mezzanine post per week in the regular content calendar
- Photography style: dark, dramatic, low-light (see Photography Style doc)
- Email template: "There's a room upstairs at Uno Más." — proven opener

### Copy Language for the Mezzanine
**Use:**
- "A room upstairs" — the discovery language
- "The Mezzanine" — always capitalize, always the full name
- "Fireplace. Leather. Private bar." — scene fragments work better than full sentences
- "Most people don't know it exists." — the intrigue hook
- "DM us to book" — the primary inquiry CTA for social
- "Call (509) 960-7989" — for direct inquiries
- "Your group deserves its own space" — the value proposition

**Never use for the Mezzanine:**
- "State-of-the-art"
- "Unforgettable experience"
- "Luxury" or "luxurious"
- Long feature lists (fireplace + private bar + dumbwaiter + leather + capacity for 37 + three packages + …)
- Prices in organic posts — take it to DM

### Inquiry Handling

2. Ask for event type, date, and approximate group size
3. Share the appropriate package once size is confirmed
4. Connect them with Karissa or Ramsey for booking confirmation

## Cross-Venue Rules

- The three spaces (Cantina, Patio, Mezzanine) are never described as the same type of space — they each have distinct identities and content strategies.
- "Uno Más" as a brand encompasses all three. "The Mezzanine" is always the Mezzanine specifically — never refer to it as just "upstairs" in public-facing content.
- When generating content, always clarify which space is being featured before writing copy or selecting photography.
- The Patio and Cantina share the Uno Más brand identity. The Mezzanine has its own separate identity within the Uno Más family.
