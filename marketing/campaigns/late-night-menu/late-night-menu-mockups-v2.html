Updated lineup, five layouts. Paloma removed; $5 pints (16oz draft, reg $7) and $4 well shots added. Margs $6 (reg $12.50), pitchers $30 (reg $50), two street tacos $10 across all five. Each artboard is a true 5.5in × 8.5in, so Print / Save as PDF gives a 5-page PDF at size.
Two things I could not resolve and did not guess.
• The well shot has no regular price anywhere in the menu docs. The only shot on record is the Latin Candy Shot at $8.50 — a six-ingredient specialty pour, not a well. So I show no struck-through price for it rather than imply a discount I can't substantiate. Every other item shows its real regular price.
• Pint size. Priced against the 16oz draft at $7. The 20oz is $8 — confirm $5 is 16oz only, or the floor will get asked and guess.
