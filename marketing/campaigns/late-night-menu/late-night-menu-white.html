5.5 × 8.5 in · no graphics · $6 margs · $5 pints · $4 shots · $30 pitchers · 2 tacos $10
All five changes applied. Pure white ground — no cream, no navy sheet. “$4 Shots” with WELL POURS ONLY underneath, so the word "well" appears once, not twice. Every illustration, starburst, spark, brush texture and icon removed. What is left is the wordmark, type, rules, and two structural colour bars at the base. The taco block is the anchor in all five — the variants differ in how it is made to stand out.
Because the ornament is gone, the type is doing everything. That is why the five differ mainly in the taco block's treatment — panel, box, scale, column weight, or tint. Worth viewing at full size: at 56% these read closer to each other than they do in the hand.

Still open: the regular well-shot price (shown with no strike-through — the only shot on record is the $8.50 Latin Candy Shot, a specialty pour), and confirmation that the $5 pint is 16oz only.
