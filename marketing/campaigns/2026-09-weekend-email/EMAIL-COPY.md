## COPY RULES THIS DRAFT HOLDS TO

- **Uno Más** always with the accent — never "Uno Mas"
- Never: "taco shop", "authentic Mexican", "mouthwatering", "culinary journey",
  "artisanal", "mixology", "leverage", "perfect for any occasion"
- Short sentences. Fragments fine. Lead with experience, prove with food
- No Happy Hour reference **except** Late Night Happy Hour — the 3–5pm one is retired
- No 21+ language anywhere — kid-friendly at all times
- Tagline is **Get a little lost.**
