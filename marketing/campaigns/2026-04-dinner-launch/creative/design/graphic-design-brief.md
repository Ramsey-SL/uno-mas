# Graphic Design Brief — Uno Más Dinner Launch
> Campaign line: "Dinner. At Uno Más."
> Written by: Creative Director
> Date: April 9, 2026
> Production tools: Canva Pro + AI image generation (Midjourney / DALL-E 3 / Adobe Firefly)

---

## Visual Direction Summary

**Mood:** Speakeasy meets dinner destination. Warm, low-light, intimate. The vibe of a friend's photo that looked incredible — editorial, not advertising.
**Color palette:** Deep black/charcoal (dominant), warm amber/copper (primary mood for this campaign), teal (Uno Más brand accent, used for logo only).
**Photography feel:** Real food shot at the bar or table — not studio, not overhead stock. Slight angle. Sauce visible. Space present. Warm single light source.
**Typography:** Bold, confident display type for price/headline. Clean minimal for supporting info. White or cream on dark backgrounds.

---

## Asset 1: Meta Feed — 1080×1080px (3 variants)

**Technical specs:** 1080×1080px | JPG or PNG | Max 30MB | Text safe zone: 80px from all edges

---

### Variant A — Surf & Turf / Price Contrast

**Subject:** Close-up of the Surf & Turf plate. Skirt steak sliced, tiger prawns alongside, cilantro lime rice, black beans. Plated beautifully on a dark plate or slate. Warm amber side-lighting from left. Sauce glistening. Shot at a slight angle — not overhead. Dark background with minimal negative space.

**Text overlay:**
- Line 1 (top third, bold white): `$47. At a taco shop.`
- Line 2 (bottom quarter, slightly smaller): `Dinner. At Uno Más.`
- Logo mark (bottom right corner, small): Uno Más alpaca in brand teal

**Color treatment:** Deep black background. Warm amber glow on the food. White text. Logo in teal.

**Canva notes:** Start from Instagram Post (Square) template. Apply dark overlay at 60% opacity over photo. Use bold display font (Playfair Display, Libre Baskerville, or similar serif) for price line. Sans-serif for "Dinner. At Uno Más."

---

### Variant B — Raw Bar / Item Revelation

**Subject:** 4–5 oysters on the half shell arranged on a dark slate board or black plate. Ice visible beneath. A single wedge of lemon. Side-lit from amber source — dramatic shadow. Intimate. One or two oysters with mignonette or hot sauce. Very close crop. No props beyond the oysters and board.

**Text overlay:**
- Line 1 (centered, large bold white): `Oysters. At Uno Más.`
- Line 2 (small, bottom): `2020 N Monroe, Spokane`
- Logo mark (top left corner, small): brand teal

**Color treatment:** Near-black dominant. Single warm light source on oysters. White text. Maximum contrast.

**Canva notes:** Minimal layout — nearly all image. The text is a revelation, not decoration. Keep type large and centered. Don't crowd the oysters.

---

### Variant C — Uno Más Feast / FOMO

**Subject:** The Feast spread on a table — multiple dishes visible from a slight overhead-angle: tacos, Surf & Turf, smoked meats, cocktails. Two sets of hands reaching in (candid, not posed). The Mezzanine atmosphere in background — leather, warm lighting, dim. Group dinner energy.

**Text overlay:**
- Line 1 (top): `The Feast. $129.`
- Line 2 (bottom): `Dinner. At Uno Más.`
- Logo mark (corner): brand teal

**Color treatment:** Warm amber glow throughout. The space should feel intimate and special. Text in white or cream.

**Canva notes:** This is the most atmosphere-forward asset. The space sells as much as the food. If using AI image gen, include The Mezzanine-style setting (leather banquette, fireplace, dim lighting).

## Asset 2: Meta Story — 1080×1920px (2 variants)

**Technical specs:** 1080×1920px | JPG or PNG | Text safe zone: 250px from top, 340px from bottom (platform UI overlay)

---

### Variant D — Price Anchor (text-dominant)

**Layout:** Near-black background, full bleed. Text-only design — the number is the visual.

**Text layout (centered, vertical stack):**
```
$47.
[16px gap]
At a taco shop.
[32px gap]
Dinner. At Uno Más.
[32px gap]
2020 N Monroe, Spokane
```

**Type:** `$47.` — very large (120–140pt), bold, white. `At a taco shop.` — medium (36–42pt), white. `Dinner. At Uno Más.` — bold (48pt), amber/copper. Address — small (22pt), muted white/cream.

**Canva notes:** No image needed. The stark text on black is the design. Add subtle amber gradient from bottom (0% to 15% opacity amber) for warmth. Optional: thin amber horizontal rule between price and "Dinner. At Uno Más."

---

### Variant E — Campaign Line (cinematic)

**Subject:** Full-bleed moody food photo (Surf & Turf preferred, or oysters) with heavy dark overlay (75–80% black).

**Text layout (centered):**
```
Dinner.
[large gap — let it breathe]
At Uno Más.
[small bottom]
Logo mark
```

**Type:** Both lines very large (80–100pt), bold white. Maximum breathing room between them. Nothing else.

**Canva notes:** Almost all text whitespace. The food photo is barely visible through the overlay — just enough to communicate richness. The two words carry everything.

## Asset 4: Email Header — 600×300px (1 asset)

**Technical specs:** 600×300px | JPG | Max 200KB (email-optimized) | No text overlay required

**Subject:** Full-bleed hero food photo — Surf & Turf plate OR the Feast spread. Warm amber, intimate, low-light. The image alone should signal "this isn't the taco menu you know." Cropped for 2:1 ratio. No logo, no text — the email headline carries the message.

**Canva notes:** Use the same Surf & Turf or Feast photo from the Meta assets. Crop to 600×300. Export at 80% quality JPG to stay under 200KB. Test in email preview.

## Asset 5: Instagram Organic Posts — 1080×1080px (3 posts)

### Post 1 — Launch Reveal (April 15)
**Subject:** The full dinner spread — multiple dishes on the table, warm atmosphere, beautiful plating. This is the hero shot that tells Spokane the dinner menu exists.
**Text overlay (optional):** `Dinner. At Uno Más.` — small, bottom-left, white. Keep it minimal.
**Direction:** Stop-the-scroll energy. The visual does the work. Don't over-design.

### Post 2 — Behind the Scenes (April 22)
**Subject:** Kitchen action — smoked meat being sliced, or a dish being plated. Real, not staged. The craft is evident.
**Text overlay:** None preferred — let the visual breathe. Caption carries the story.
**Direction:** Documentary style. Authentic. This builds credibility for the dinner program.

### Post 3 — The Feast (April 27)
**Subject:** The Feast spread from above or at slight angle. Multiple dishes. Hands optional. The Mezzanine atmosphere in background.
**Text overlay (optional):** `$129. Feeds 2–3. Your move.` — small text, corner placement, amber or white.
**Direction:** Aspirational and social — "I want to bring people here." Group energy.
