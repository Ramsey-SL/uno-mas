# KPI Framework — Uno Más Dinner Launch

> Campaign: 2026-04-dinner-launch
> Goal: Drive dinner covers and establish Uno Más as Spokane's dinner destination
> Dates: April 15 → May 21, 2026
> Budget: $2,000 total / $1,500 paid media

---

## Primary KPIs

These are the 3 numbers that define whether this campaign succeeded.

| Metric | Definition | Baseline | Target | +/- vs. Baseline | Platform |
|--------|-----------|---------|--------|-----------------|---------|
| Dinner covers (dinner ticket count) | Toast POS dinner-hour tickets during campaign window | 0 (new menu) | 150+ covers over 6 weeks | New baseline | Toast POS |
| Meta ROAS | Campaign-attributed revenue ÷ Meta ad spend | No prior data | 3.0x minimum | New baseline | Meta Ads Manager + GA4 |
| Email open rate (launch send) | Opens ÷ delivered on dinner launch email | ~25% list avg | 35%+ | +10 pts | Klaviyo |

**How baselines were set**: Dinner menu is net-new — no prior baseline exists. Toast dinner-hour ticket count from pre-launch period (pre-April 15) should be pulled as the comparison baseline. Meta ROAS target of 3x is conservative for a local restaurant with tight geographic targeting. Email 35% target reflects launch announcement premium over everyday campaign avg.

## Attribution

**Official reporting model**: Last-click via GA4
**Reporting platform**: GA4 + Klaviyo + Toast POS (triangulate)
**In-platform optimization**: Meta Ads Manager ROAS used for bid decisions only

**Cross-channel note**: At this budget tier, cross-channel deduplication is less complex — Meta and email are the only paid/tracked touchpoints. GA4 last-click is the source of truth. Expect Meta Ads Manager to overreport by 20–40% vs. GA4 due to view-through attribution.

**Toast POS tracking**: Dinner covers are the truest measure of success. Pull dinner-hour (5pm+) ticket counts weekly from Toast and compare to pre-campaign baseline. This is the business metric that matters most.

## Target-Setting Math

**Revenue goal for 6-week campaign**: $8,000–$12,000 in dinner revenue (est.)
- Average dinner check: ~$55/person (Surf & Turf $47 + 1 cocktail avg)
- Covers needed for $10K revenue: ~182 covers
- Target covers: 150+ (conservative) / 200+ (stretch)

**Meta paid math (at $1,500 spend):**
- Target CPC: $2.00
- Expected clicks: 750 clicks
- Expected landing page CVR to visit: 5% (local, warm audience)
- Implied dinner reservations / intent actions from paid: ~37
- ROAS at $55 avg check × 37 covers: $2,035 ÷ $1,500 = 1.36x paid ROAS alone

**Assessment**: Paid ROAS alone will not hit 3x at $1,500. That's expected — and correct. The 3x ROAS target includes email and organic social attribution. **Owned channels (email + SMS + organic) are the primary drivers at this budget.** Meta paid is the awareness and retargeting layer, not the sole revenue engine. Blended campaign ROAS of 3x+ is realistic when owned channels are factored in.
