# Creative Brief — Uno Más Dinner Launch

> From: Campaign Strategist
> To: Creative Director
> Campaign: 2026-04-dinner-launch
> Date: April 9, 2026

---

## Strategic Context

Uno Más has soft-launched a full dinner menu — Surf & Turf, Raw Bar, Carne Asada, and a $129 Feast for the table — and the early response is positive. The campaign's job is to shift the audience's mental model: Uno Más is no longer just a taco shop — it's Spokane's best dinner option. The audience already knows and likes Uno Más. We're not convincing them it's good. We're telling them it just got a whole lot better.

---

## The Core Message
> Uno Más just became Spokane's best dinner spot — and most people don't know it yet.

---

## Audience

- **Who**: Spokane locals 25–45, experience planners, already Uno Más-aware
- **Journey stage**: Consideration → Decision
- **Key belief to create**: "Uno Más isn't just for tacos — it's a legitimate dinner destination"
- **Psychological lever**: **Pull** — the surprise of the menu IS the hook. Lead with revelation, not reassurance.

---

## Tone for This Campaign

Unmistakably Uno Más voice — group-chat energy, confident, a little chaotic. But allow a slight elevation for the dinner context. Think: "So we did a thing… and it involves oysters." Intrigue first. Let the food do the talking. No adjective stacking. No fine-dining pretension.

**One-liner to test all creative against**: *Would the alpaca say this in a backwards baseball cap? Good. Would a sommelier say it? Too far.*

---

## Proof Point to Lead With

Soft launch validation: the food exists, people tried it, people loved it. Now it's the audience's turn. Use this as the FOMO lever — not "we're launching," but "this has been happening and you haven't been there yet."

---

## What to Avoid

- "Authentic," "artisanal," "culinary journey," "mouthwatering" ❌
- Adjective stacking ("fresh, delicious, flavorful") ❌
- Fine-dining language or stuffy positioning ❌
- Generic food photography captions — let the visual do the work ❌
- Overcrowded copy — one hook per ad, not three ❌

## Creative Requirements

### Paid — Meta Ads

| Format | Specs | Quantity | Priority | Notes |
|--------|-------|----------|----------|-------|
| Static image — Feed | 1080×1080 | 3 variants | HIGH | Each leads with a different menu item: (1) Surf & Turf, (2) Uno Más Feast, (3) Raw Bar / Oysters |
| Static image — Story/Reel | 1080×1920 | 2 variants | HIGH | Text-forward — one price anchor hook, one "we did a thing" reveal |
| Static image — Facebook Feed | 1200×628 | 2 variants | MEDIUM | Repurpose best 2 of the 1080×1080 variants, cropped |

**Ad copy direction for each variant** (full copy from Ad Copy agent — these are creative direction anchors):
- Variant A (Surf & Turf): Lead with the price contrast — $47 at a taco shop hits different. Skirt steak + tiger prawns.
- Variant B (Uno Más Feast): Lead with the social proof hook — "dinner for two just got a lot more interesting." $129, feeds 2–3.
- Variant C (Raw Bar): Lead with surprise — "Oysters. At Uno Más. Yes, really." Short. Let the visual carry it.

### Email — Klaviyo

| Format | Specs | Quantity | Notes |
|--------|-------|----------|-------|
| Launch announcement email | 600px wide, mobile-first | 1 | Loyal Uno Más Rewards: The Cantina Club list; subject line options needed (3 variants); preview text critical |
| Follow-up email (Week 3) | 600px wide | 1 | Different angle — focus on Feast or Raw Bar; second touch for non-openers |

**Email creative direction**:
- Subject line direction: Intrigue > announcement. "So we did a thing" energy. Not "Introducing Our New Dinner Menu."
- Hero: Full-bleed food photo (Surf & Turf or Feast — whichever is most visually striking)
- Body: Short. 3–4 sentences. One menu feature. One price anchor. One CTA.
- CTA: "See the dinner menu" or "Get in here" — not "Learn more"

### SMS — Klaviyo

| Format | Character limit | Quantity | Send timing |
|--------|----------------|----------|------------|
| Launch SMS | 160 chars | 1 | Apr 15, 6pm (dinner hour) |
| Week 3 follow-up SMS | 160 chars | 1 | May 1, 5:30pm |

**SMS creative direction**: Maximum 2 sentences. Lead with the hook. Link to menu. Emojis: 1 max (🌮 or 🥩). No "we're excited to announce."

### Organic Social — Instagram (Vista Social)

| Format | Quantity | Schedule | Notes |
|--------|----------|----------|-------|
| Launch post (feed) | 1 | Apr 15 | Big reveal — the full dinner menu. Multiple menu items in carousel or single hero shot. |
| Behind-the-scenes / kitchen | 1 | Apr 22 | Show the process — house-smoked, plated, made for real. Builds credibility. |
| Social proof / UGC | 1 | Apr 27 | Customer reaction, early reviews, or quote. "Didn't expect this from Uno Más" energy. |

**Caption direction**: Short. Punchy. 1–2 lines + hashtags. No paragraph-length copy on social. Use the FOMO angle — "this has been happening. your move."
