# Love Island Watch Party — Promo Video Creative Brief

## One-sentence summary
A summer rooftop social event where friends gather at The Mezzanine for cocktails, games, laughter, and all the Love Island drama.

## Core concept
A group of friends around a communal table on **The Mezzanine rooftop patio** playing a social party game (a stacking/balance challenge with tall colorful drink towers / game pieces) while enjoying cocktails. Raised towers in celebration, reactions, laughter, friendly competition.

**Takeaway:** "This isn't just watching Love Island. It's a night out with friends." → positions the night as a **weekly social event**, not passive TV viewing.

## Visual elements
- **Setting:** The Mezzanine at Uno Más — outdoor rooftop/lounge, patio seating, umbrellas + heaters, casual summer-evening atmosphere.
- **Activity:** guests around a large communal table; stacking/balance challenge with colorful drink towers; celebratory raises; reactions + friendly competition.
- **F&B:** large colorful cocktails featured prominently; drinks become part of the game; playful, social, Instagram-worthy presentation.

## Tone
Fun · social · playful · competitive · energetic · inclusive. (Girls-night / group-night / date-night.)

## Messaging themes
Friendship (come as a group) · Participation (not just watching) · Celebration (cheers/toasts/raised glasses) · Summer escape (rooftop = vacation energy) · Shared drama (Love Island as social backdrop).

## Creative keywords
Villa Crew · Summer Nights · Rooftop Energy · Cocktails · Tequila · Group Experience · Friendly Competition · Watch Party · Reality TV · Social Event · Girls Night · Date Night · Thursday Tradition.

## Art-direction rules (apply to ALL Love Island creative — poster, social, web)
**KEEP:** bright social atmosphere · groups of friends · elevated cocktails · interactive energy · packed-house feel · summer rooftop ambiance.
**AVOID:** empty lounge imagery · generic luxury resort visuals · overly dark nightclub aesthetics · pool-party-only imagery.
**BETTER:** The Mezzanine filled with friend-groups holding cocktails, laughing/cheering, watching Love Island on a big screen — emphasize the **social experience over the TV show.**
