Campaign Brief — Love Island Watch Party

Type: event (recurring) Status: 🟢 building (poster finalized · Resy wired · FB event drafted) Owner: Ramsey
Dates: Thursdays Jun 25 · Jul 2 · Jul 9 · Season Finale Sun Jul 12 · Doors 5PM / Show 6PM Venue: The Mezzanine
Last updated: 2026-06-20

1. Objective

Drive midweek (Thursday) traffic into The Mezzanine by owning "where Spokane watches Love Island" —
a recurring social viewing party that fills the upstairs on an otherwise quiet night and builds a
returning weekly crowd. Primary action: show up Thursday (RSVP/walk-in) + order drinks.

2. The offer / hook

Weekly Love Island watch party in the speakeasy upstairs — big screens, signature cocktails, tacos &
shareables, a room full of people yelling at the TV together. Doors 5PM, show 6PM. Reservations highly
recommended. Hook line: "All tacos. All tequila. All drama. Bring your villa crew."
Season runs 3 Thursdays (Jun 25 / Jul 2 / Jul 9) + a Season Finale watch party Sun Jul 12.

3. Audience

  • Primary: adults 21–35, social, reality-TV fans, the "where are we going Thursday?" group-chat crowd — skews friend-groups and date-duos who want a plan, not just a bar.
  • Secondary: existing regulars looking for a midweek reason; Cantina Club members (cross-promote).
  • JTBD: "give me a fun standing Thursday plan with my people + good drinks."

4. Key messages (on-brand — confident, playful, a little chaotic)

  • "Spokane's Love Island HQ. Upstairs. Thursdays."
  • "Grab your people, grab a marg, come yell at the TV with us."
  • "The Mezzanine + Love Island + craft cocktails = your new Thursday." (Get a little lost.)
  • Mezzanine framing stays cool/in-the-know, but watch-party tone leans playful/social.
  • Always: 21+, The Mezzanine (never "upstairs bar"), craft cocktails (never "mixology").

5. Channels & cadence

  • Instagram/TikTok (primary): weekly Thursday-morning reminder reel/post + story countdown sticker; recap clips after.
  • Klaviyo email + SMS: weekly Thursday "tonight!" nudge to the 21+/Cantina Club segment.
  • In-store: table tents + a Mezzanine sign during the week.
  • Website: events listing + homepage event callout (see §7).
  • Facebook: recurring FB Event (Thursdays) + Finale event — see facebook-event.md.

6. Collateral needed

  • Social: 1 announcement reel + weekly reminder post template + story countdown + 2–3 recap clips. (Footage exists — DAM tags:mezzanine AND tags:watchparty = the MezzWatchParty set.)
  • Email + SMS: 1 reusable weekly template (swap the episode/date).
  • In-store: table tent + Mezzanine A-frame/sign.
  • Web: event card + homepage banner.

7. Website integration

  • Add a recurring event to the Mezzanine / Events page: "Love Island Watch Party — Thursdays."
  • Homepage event callout (the site already supports event-forward Mezzanine framing).
  • CTA: Reserve a table on Resy (same as every Uno Más reservation) → https://resy.com/cities/spokane-wa/venues/uno-mas-tacos-and-tequila · walk-ins welcome.

8. KPIs

Thursday Mezzanine covers + bar sales (Toast), watch-party headcount week-over-week (returning crowd),
social engagement on the reels, email/SMS click→visit, new Cantina Club signups at the event.

9. Open inputs (mostly resolved via the poster — remaining)

✅ Dates: Thu Jun 25 / Jul 2 / Jul 9 + Finale Sun Jul 12 · ✅ Doors 5 / Show 6 · ✅ Reservations recommended
· ✅ Food/drink: signature cocktails + tacos & shareables.

Creative direction (canonical — see video-creative-brief.md)

Summer rooftop social event, not passive TV. KEEP: packed-house friend-groups, elevated cocktails,

A summer rooftop social event where friends gather at The Mezzanine for cocktails, games, laughter, and all the Love Island drama.
