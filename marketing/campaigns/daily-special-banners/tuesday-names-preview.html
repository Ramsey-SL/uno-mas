Seen at size, two things change my read.
`Street Taco Tuesday` is long — it fills the tile and pushes toward two lines on narrow screens, which weakens the headline. Still the cheapest improvement, but it costs you some presence.
`One More Tuesday` sets beautifully — three short words, balanced, and the same character count as the current name, so it drops in without any layout change at all. It also reads as a brand rather than a category, which is the whole point.
`Second Taco's Free` confirms the worry — as a headline it reads like a coupon, not a programme. Good SMS line, wrong tile.
Still my recommendation: keep `Taco Tuesday` unless you pick `One More Tuesday`. Everything else is lateral, and lateral isn't worth reprinting table tents for.
