Recommend edge 2, the ticket. It's the only one where the border means something — punch-out notches say "this is a deal you tear off," which is exactly what the tile is. It also stays legible at any width and needs no extra colour. Edge 4 is louder if you want maximum pop; edge 3 brings the most colour but competes with the pink prices for attention.
On the animation: the sequence deliberately finishes with the offer rows, not the tile. The tile arriving is just movement; the rows sliding in one at a time is what makes someone actually read them. Total ~2s, and it runs once per page load — not a loop.
Note the load order: the tile resolves before the hero, so the special is the first thing that stops moving. That's the whole argument for the above-hero slot.
