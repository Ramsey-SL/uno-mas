On colour: G3 (pink ground) is the one I'd push. Pink is your stated hero colour and right now it only ever appears as an accent — as a full ground it's the most unmistakably Uno Más option here, and nothing else on Monroe looks like it. G2 (navy/yellow) is the safe high-contrast pick. E2 is the answer if the priority is purely making the items win.
Avoid G4 — navy on teal has less contrast than it appears, and the subtext suffers.
A thought on using colour as information: you could give each day its own ground — Tuesday pink, Wednesday navy, Thursday teal, Late Night deep navy. Regulars would learn the colour before they read a word. The cost is losing one consistent look, so it's a real trade either way.
