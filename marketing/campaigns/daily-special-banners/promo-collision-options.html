When a weekly promo lands on a day-of-week special

Real case · Gift Card promo (Thu–Sun) × Big F’N Thursday
The situation, using this week's actual collision. The gift card promo runs Thursday through Sunday. Big F’N Thursday runs every Thursday. On Thursday both want the same slot above the hero.
The principle each option is really arguing about: the weekly promo is news — it expires, so it has to be seen now. The day special is a standing offer — it'll be there next week too. Options B, C and D all say the news gets the hero and the standing offer gets a reference. Option A says they're equals.
One thing worth deciding before the design. A guest reading two offers immediately asks whether they stack. Here they do — a $10 quesadilla counts toward the $50. Every option below has a place to say so, and saying it is worth more than the layout choice. If two offers ever don't stack, that has to be on the tile, not left for the server to explain at the table.

Costs youThe pink bar fights the pink starburst directly beneath it, and it competes with the site's own announcement bar for the same visual job. A single line also can't hold a qualifier, so “proteins extra” and “menu cocktails only” get dropped — exactly the wording that prevents arguments at the table.
What this needs in code, whichever you pick. Right now the three day tiles each gate themselves independently and none of them knows a weekly promo exists. Collision handling means one thing has to decide what renders — a single slot component that asks “is a weekly promo live, and is there also a day special today?” and then picks solo tile, or collision layout. That's the same argument as driving all of this off site_events instead of hardcoded config: the collision case is where hardcoding stops being merely untidy and starts being wrong.

Not shown, and it's the harder one: Friday and Saturday, Late Night Happy Hour (8–10pm) overlaps the gift card promo and needs an hour gate. Three offers, one slot. Worth settling the two-offer case first.
