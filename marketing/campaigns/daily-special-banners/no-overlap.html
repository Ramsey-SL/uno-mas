Art without overlap

Three structural fixes · nothing sits behind text
I was wrong to keep tuning opacity. Line art behind text doesn't work at any opacity — the taco art is dense and high-contrast, so at 5% it's still enough to break the prices, and at 2% it isn't worth having. The fix is structural: give the art its own zone, or remove it.
All three below have zero overlap between art and type. Same offers, same palette, ticket border kept.
HNo art — the border does the work Papel picado strip along the top, dashed ticket border, punch notches. Nothing behind the text at all, so every price is at full contrast. Simplest and the most reliable.

Recommend I — art in its own panel. It's the only option where you gain something by fixing the problem: the line art goes from an unreadable 5% smudge to a full-strength graphic, because it's on navy instead of behind type. Text is at full contrast, and swapping the panel image is how you change days.
H is the safest — no art, no risk, and the papel picado plus ticket border carry enough interest on their own. J works but it's the tallest, which is a real cost for something that runs above the hero 52 weeks a year.
The general rule, now recorded: in this tile, art never goes behind text. It gets its own zone or it doesn't appear.
