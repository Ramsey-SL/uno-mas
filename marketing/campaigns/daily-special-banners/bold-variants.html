Recommend A, with C as the mobile fallback. A is the boldest without abandoning the flyer language — the hard navy outline and offset shadow make each offer read as a physical sticker, which is exactly the printed-poster energy the tile is built on. The one-at-a-time slam gives you the "jump out and make you read it" without looping forever. C is more legible and stacks better on a phone, so it may be worth running C below ~700px and A above.
Avoid D for a tile that shows every week — continuous motion is attention-grabbing once and irritating by the fourth Tuesday. B is the most striking single image but it drops the sticker metaphor the rest of your print work uses.

⚠️ "Late Night Happy Hour" needs a ruling. Your repo currently says "Happy Hour (3–5pm) and the $12 lunch special are retired — do not reference them." Reintroducing the words risks a guest hearing "happy hour" and expecting the old 3–5pm program. The upside is real though — "happy hour" instantly communicates "discounted drinks" with no explanation needed, which "Late Night" alone doesn't. If you want it, I'll narrow the rule to: the standalone 3–5pm Happy Hour stays retired; "Late Night Happy Hour" is a distinct named program — and brief staff on the difference, since they'll be the ones asked.
