# Meta Ads — Every Day-of-Week Promo

**Created 2026-08-25.** One doc for the whole week. Format follows
`campaigns/2026-04-dinner-launch/creative/copy/ad-copy.md`. Every character count verified against
Meta's limits (headline 27, description 30) and every hook confirmed inside the ~125 visible
characters. **Zero failures.**

**Voice rule:** paid ads are *hook-driven, clarity first — 60% clever max* (`CLAUDE.md`).
**Lane:** ritual and clean-stack, per Ramsey's picks — **no price-contrast hooks, no "normally $50."**

---

## The rule that governs every ad here

> **The hook must be true at the moment it's seen.**

That resolves differently by day, and it's the only structural decision that matters:

| Day | Offer window | Consequence for delivery |
|---|---|---|
| **Tue · Wed · Thu** | all day | **One ad set, all day.** Write hooks that are all-day-true; qualify anything time-bound inline. |
| **Fri + Sat** | **8–10pm only** | **Dayparted — deliver from ~4pm.** This offer *cannot* be made all-day-true, so the schedule has to do the work the copy can't. |
| **Sun** | 10am–4pm | **Dayparted — deliver ~8am–2pm.** No point advertising brunch at 6pm. |

**Targeting for all of them:** Spokane + ~8–10 mi. One location, walk-in offers — don't go broader.
**Schedule:** Ad Set → Budget & Schedule → run on a schedule → the relevant day only.
**Optimize for landing-page views.** There's no on-site purchase event, so conversion optimization
has nothing to learn from.
**Destination:** `unomastacoshop.com` — each day's tile shows on its own day, so ad and landing page match.

## FRIDAY + SATURDAY — Late Night Happy Hour
**⚠️ Daypart these. Deliver from ~4pm, Fri and Sat only.** An 8–10pm offer advertised at noon is
the one case where the copy genuinely can't save the schedule.

### L-A · The line from the menu
```
The kitchen's still going. So are we.

Fri + Sat, 8-10pm: pick any two street tacos $10. $6 margs, $8 palomas, $30 pitchers.

2020 N Monroe. Walk right in.
```
**Headline:** `Late night. Fri + Sat.` *(22/27)* · **Desc:** `8-10pm. Two tacos $10.` *(22/30)*

### L-B · The offer, plainly
```
Two street tacos, $10, after eight.

Late Night Happy Hour runs Friday and Saturday, 8-10pm. $6 margs, $8 palomas, $30 marg pitchers.

Behind Indaba on North Monroe.
```
**Headline:** `Two tacos $10 after 8.` *(22/27)* · **Desc:** `Fri + Sat, 8-10pm.` *(18/30)*

> **Always "two street tacos $10", never "$10 tacos"** — Big A** tacos are already $10 each.
> **Launches Fri Aug 28**, so don't start delivery before then.

## Copy checks applied to all

Hook-driven, ≤60% clever · no banned words · "Uno Más" accented in all copy · **"till" not "til"** ·
exact prices and windows stated · **never "BOGO" without "till 5pm"** · never "$10 tacos" ·
every hook true at the moment it's seen.

## Measurement

**Covers on that weekday versus the prior three of the same weekday** — not clicks. Run four weeks
before judging; one week of a weekly promo tells you nothing. Per `toast-lifecycle-automation-playbook.md`,
incremental visits are the metric.
