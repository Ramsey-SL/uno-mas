Built at true trim size — every artboard is exactly 5.5in × 8.5in, so “Print / Save as PDF” gives you a real 5-page PDF at size, one option per page. On screen they're shown at 62%. Toggle the safe area to see the 0.25in margin nothing important should cross.
Print resolution — this is the piece where it matters. Only three photos in the whole library are genuinely safe at this size, and I used those: WeekendSpecial_Portrait (3506×4381), FoodOnTable_FINAL (2541×2560), TacoCloseUpV10_FINAL (2560×2135). A half page at 300 dpi needs 1650×2550px.
I deliberately did not use any of the June 20260623_* photos — they're 1536×2048, which is about 240 dpi here. That's the shared-album problem, and a table topper is exactly where it would show.

Logo public_id gotcha: the wordmarks are addressed as uno-mas/website/logos/… even though they physically sit in approved-assets/logos. Using the folder path 404s. They're 3077×1577 transparent PNGs — plenty at 1.85in.

Before this goes to the printer, three things need your call:
• Terms. I wrote “Gift card is handed to you at checkout — not applied to the same visit. While supplies last.” I inferred the first half from how you described it and the second from having 200 cards. Is there a one-per-guest or one-per-visit limit? If so it belongs on the card, not in a server's explanation.
• Expiry. Nothing on these says when the gift cards themselves expire, because I don't know. If they do expire, that has to be printed.
• Stock and finish. These are designed single-sided. A table topper usually wants a tent fold or a stand — tell me which and I'll produce the second panel or a folding version.
