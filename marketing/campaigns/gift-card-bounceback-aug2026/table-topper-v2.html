Rebuilt on the system from your references, not from scratch. Same anatomy as the Weekend Special piece: navy masthead with the script wordmark and starbursts bleeding off both corners → rough-edged pink headline block → illustrated art with the offer beside it → yellow brush price blocks → blue notched ribbon → script tagline with a hand underline → pink “Ask your server.” bar → navy “Tacos. Margs. Brunch. Get a little lost.” footer.
Where the illustrated art came from. I cannot generate images, so I did not try to fake those illustrations. Instead: your Weekend Special topper is already in Cloudinary at full print resolution — 20260814_UM_PROMO_WeekendSpecial_Portrait, 3506×4381 PNG — so I cropped the margarita glasses and the chip & dip platter straight out of it and feathered the edges so they sit on cream invisibly. Same artwork, same resolution, reused. The masthead is the real um-t-t-nooutline-white-asset-2 wordmark (2691×864).

What this means: a genuinely new subject still needs your image generator. But anything built from margs, chips & dips, wings, nachos or masa fries can be assembled from art you already own at print resolution.
