1. Objective

Build standing midweek rituals that drive Tue–Thu covers (the slower nights) and give Spokane a
reason to pick Uno Más midweek. Primary action: dine-in covers + drink attach on the named day.

Day Special

  • Primary: Spokane locals + after-work crowd looking for a midweek go-to; groups and friend-duos.

  • JTBD: "give me an easy, worth-it midweek plan" — a ritual, not a coupon.

4. Key messages (on-brand)

Frame as a vibe/ritual, not a discount fire-sale (brand = experience over deals, price-confident):
- "Taco Tuesday hits different at 2020 N Monroe." (BOGO street tacos · $6 margs · $30 pitchers)
- "$5 Wednesday: $5 pints, $5 street tacos. Math we can get behind."
- "Burrito Thursday — $15 burritos & bowls. Your Thursday just got a plan."
- Tie-line: "Three reasons to get a little lost midweek."
- Voice: confident, playful; never apologetic about price; lead with the experience, prove with the food.

5. Channels & cadence

  • Instagram/TikTok: weekly recurring day-of posts/stories (3/week) — a templated set per day.
  • In-store: table tents + menu insert/board listing all three days.
  • Klaviyo email/SMS: a "your midweek lineup" announcement + light weekly nudges.
  • Website: a Weekly Specials section + homepage callout.

6. Collateral needed

  • Social: 3 day-templates (Taco Tue / $5 Wed / Burrito Thu) — reusable, each with food/drink hero from the DAM.
    Pull: tags:food (tacos, burritos, bowls), tags:cocktails (margs), beer for $5 Wed.
  • In-store: one table tent (all 3 days) + a specials board graphic.
  • Email/SMS: 1 announcement + reusable weekly templates.
  • Web: Weekly Specials page/section + homepage banner.

7. Website integration

  • New "Weekly Specials" section (or menu callout) on unomastacoshop.com listing all three days.
  • Homepage banner rotating the day's special. CTA: directions / reserve / order.

8. KPIs

Tue/Wed/Thu cover counts vs. baseline (Toast), special-item sales (taco/burrito/marg/pint units),
midweek traffic lift, social engagement, email/SMS → visit.

9. Open inputs needed

  1. Time windows per day (lunch-only vs all-day vs dinner) + dine-in/takeout.
  2. (resolved) Happy Hour is retired — the day-specials replace it; no overlap to manage.
  3. Start date / is this live now?
