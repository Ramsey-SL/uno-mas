Option 4 — how big should the item labels be?

Same tile · 5 steps of label size
The problem: at 40px price against a 10.5px label, the ratio is nearly 4:1 — so the numbers read and the items don't. You know what $6 is; you can't tell what it's for.
The trade: every point the label gains, the price gives up some dominance. These five steps show where that balance lands. Ratio shown on each.
Also fixed: the labels were uppercase at .12em tracking, which is the hardest possible setting to read small. Tracking tightens as size grows.
