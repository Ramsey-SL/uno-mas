6 options · live every Tuesday · shown at real size
Built against the live site's actual announcement bar — 40px tall, sticky at top, Montserrat 600, 12px mobile / 13px desktop, uppercase, letter-spacing .08em, white on #E22690, with a desktop/mobile text swap and an underlined CTA. Your current bar runs the Sunday brunch message.
Copy is consistent across all six: BOGO street tacos til 5pm (it's a lunch offer), $6 margs and $30 pitchers all day. Each option shows desktop and mobile, with a fake header and page edge beneath so you can judge it in context.
⚠️ Mobile copy had to be cut. At 390px the desktop string wraps and overflows a 40px bar — so mobile drops to 🌮 BOGO tacos til 5pm + Menu → at 11px/.05em tracking with nowrap. Whichever option you pick, the mobile string has a hard ceiling of roughly 30 characters including the CTA.
⚠️ One thing to decide regardless of which you pick: this replaces the brunch bar on Tuesdays, or stacks below it. Stacking two 40px bars eats 80px above the fold on mobile — I'd swap, not stack.

My recommendation: B, then F. F is the upgrade if you want the food-forward direction on the site too, but it triples the bar height and adds an above-the-fold image request, so it's a real performance decision rather than a style one.
