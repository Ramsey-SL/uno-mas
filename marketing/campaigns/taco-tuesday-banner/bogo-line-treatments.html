The BOGO line — 6 treatments

Fixing the two-headline collision
The problem: "BOGO Street Tacos till 5PM" was set in Antonio uppercase, directly under "Taco Tuesday" in Antonio uppercase. Same typeface, same case, similar weight — so they read as two competing headlines instead of a name and an offer.
The principle: the tile has three jobs — name (Taco Tuesday), offer (BOGO till 5PM), prices ($6/$30). Each needs a visibly different treatment or the eye can't rank them. Prices already own "yellow box," so the offer needs its own lane.
1Pink bandOffer gets its own container in the hero colour, so it sits clearly between the navy name and the yellow prices. Three tiers, three treatments. Montserrat drops it out of headline territory entirely.

Recommend 4, then 1. 4 solves it by reclassifying the line — BOGO stops being type competing with a headline and becomes the lead offer box, pink against the yellow supporting ones. It also adds no new pattern to the tile. 1 is the runner-up if you want the offer stated as a sentence rather than a chip.
