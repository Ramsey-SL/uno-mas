## What This Is

The Engraving Series is a secondary visual layer built on top of the Uno Más core brand identity. It is an illustration system — not a logo change, not a rebrand. The primary logo, wordmark, and brand colors remain untouched. This system gives the brand a rich, handcrafted visual vocabulary that can live alongside the logo in menus, on merchandise, in social content, and in print.

Think of it the way a great restaurant brand works: the logo is the signature, the illustration system is the personality.

---

## The Style

**Single-ink engraving / copper etching style.** Fine crosshatch linework. Botanical illustration meets western americana. Each subject is rendered with depth through layered hatching — no flat fills, no gradients, no solid color blocks. Everything is built from lines.

**References:**
- The April 2026 Uno Más menu (the agave trio, avocado, chips bowl, taco illustrations)
- Vintage seed catalog and botanical print illustration
- 19th century western americana woodcuts
- Sendero Provisions, P&Co brand illustration systems
- Flash tattoo linework at a finer gauge

**What it is NOT:**
- It is not clip art
- It is not flat vector illustration (no solid fills)
- It is not photographic or 3D rendered
- It is not the logo — the primary Uno Más wordmark is always kept separate and intact

## Color Usage in This System

### Primary: Single Ink — Navy
- **Hex:** `#003366`
- **Pantone:** PMS 289 C
- **CMYK:** 100 / 67 / 0 / 44
- **Background:** Cream / Natural (`#F5F0E8` or unbleached natural fabric)
- **Use:** All line art illustrations in their base form

### Accent: Brand Pink (Use Sparingly)
- **Hex:** `#E22690`
- **Pantone:** PMS 233 C
- When a second ink is added to a single illustration (e.g., pink wash on an agave, pink fill on a taco), it creates a premium 2-color variant
- Never use more than 2 inks in this style system — the restraint is the point

### Accent: Brand Blue (Use Sparingly)
- **Hex:** `#18BCDC`
- Same rule as Pink — one accent ink max

### Reverse: White on Dark
- White linework on Navy (`#003366`) or Black (`#000000`) backgrounds works beautifully for dark-garment applications
- The menu style is Navy on Cream. For black tees, invert to White on Black.

## Relationship to the Primary Logo

**Rule: The Engraving Series never replaces the logo. It complements it.**

The primary Uno Más wordmark (script + "TACOS & TEQUILA" in caps) is the brand mark. It should be used per existing brand standards — on signage, packaging, business cards, and anywhere the full brand identity is required.

The illustration system is used:
- **On menus** as decorative elements and subject illustrations
- **On merchandise** as the primary graphic (with a small logo mark as secondary placement)
- **On social content** as visual texture, background elements, and standalone icons
- **On print collateral** as section dividers, decorative borders, and hero art
- **On stickers** as standalone die-cut products

### What About a Badge Lockup?
A lockup combining the Uno Más script with a simple oval or circular border frame + a small illustration is being explored for merchandise (hat front, sticker, patch). This is a **companion lockup** — an adapted version for product-specific use — and should not replace the primary wordmark in any brand context. Treat it like a sub-brand mark for the merch line.

> ⚠️ **Logo integrity note:** Any change to the primary Uno Más script wordmark (modifying letter forms, combining it into new lockups for permanent brand use) should be done with proper graphic design review. The AI-generated badge explorations are starting points for direction — final lockups should be refined by a professional designer in vector format before being treated as the official mark.

## Production Specifications

### Screen Print (Apparel)
- **Ink colors:** 1 (navy) or 2 (navy + accent)
- **File format:** Vector (AI / EPS / SVG) — art must be converted to true vector paths, not placed raster images
- **Halftone:** Not needed — pure linework style, no halftones required
- **Minimum line weight at print size:** 0.5pt (anything thinner may drop out)

### Embroidery (Hats, Patches)
- **Simplification required:** Fine crosshatch linework cannot be directly embroidered — the design needs to be adapted to flat color fills with defined stitch zones
- **Thread colors:** 1–3 (navy main, cream background, optional pink accent)
- **Minimum element size:** 0.25" at final size
- **Process:** Use AI-generated art as direction brief for an embroidery digitizer to re-interpret

### Die-Cut Stickers
- **File:** High-res PNG at 300dpi minimum, or vector
- **Background:** White or clear (specify at order)
- **Finish:** Matte preferred (matches the engraving aesthetic); gloss is also fine
- **Bleed:** 0.125" beyond cut line

### DTG (Direct to Garment)
- **File:** High-res PNG, 300dpi at print size, sRGB color mode
- **Note:** DTG can reproduce the fine linework faithfully — best option for complex, detailed versions of the illustrations
