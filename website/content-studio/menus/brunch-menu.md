# Uno Más — Sunday Brunch Menu

**Every Sunday · 10am–4pm**  
**Status:** Approved content source for the expanded two-sided print menu  
**Updated:** July 2026

This document is the working source of truth for the expanded Sunday Brunch menu. It combines the approved brunch items with only the selected lunch-menu items listed below.

> Website source of truth remains Supabase `menu_sections` / `menu_items`, daypart `brunch` (project `coandmppuqqzcbbhcien`). Live menu: `/menu?tab=brunch`.

## Global menu notes

- Tacos are **street size only** with a **two-taco minimum**.
- **Batata may be substituted as the vegetarian protein option for any item offering a protein choice.**
- Current approved print-menu direction: **8.5 × 11 in, front and back, CMYK, 300 dpi**.
- Display name must always be written **Uno Más** with the accent.

## Visual requirements

- White print-efficient background.
- Uno Más brand navy, pink, and electric blue for typography and food line art.
- Bright beach/patio accent illustrations may use supporting non-brand colors.
- High legibility with strong hierarchy, generous spacing, and restaurant-menu typography.
- Preserve the established illustrated brunch-menu aesthetic; do not redesign the logo or core color system.
